# Landing Page Copy

## Hero

**Headline:** Understand EB-2 NIW before you begin

**Value proposition:** Learn the eligibility requirements, how to explain and support your planned work, and what an NIW petition means for your path to residency.

## Accordion

**Intro:** Explore five points to understand before preparing an NIW petition.

1. Check the EB-2 requirements first
2. Explain your planned work and its wider importance
3. Show how your experience supports your future plan
4. Explain why the United States should grant the waiver
5. Petition approval does not grant residency or work permission
