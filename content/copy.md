# Landing Page Copy

## Hero

**Headline:** Understand EB-2 NIW before applying

**Value proposition:** Learn about EB-2 eligibility, evidence, the waiver requirements, and the limits of petition approval.

## Accordion

**Intro:** Explore five points to understand before preparing an NIW petition.

1. Check EB-2 eligibility requirements
2. Explain your planned work's broader importance
3. Connect past contributions to future plans
4. Explain the national benefit of the waiver
5. Understand what petition approval does not grant

## Contact Form

**Intro:** Share your questions about eligibility, evidence, or the NIW process.

## Editorial Note

**Weakest headline:** 5. “Understand what petition approval does not grant” is concise but leaves the specific limits unnamed.

**Alternatives:**

- Petition approval alone grants neither residency nor work permission
- Understand why petition approval is not permission to work
