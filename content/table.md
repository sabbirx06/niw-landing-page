# EB-2 NIW compared with EB-1A

How the two categories compare on six points, plus the visa queue. Figures were read from USCIS sources on Nov 15, 2026.

| Dimension | EB-2 NIW | EB-1A | Source and date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Eligibility threshold | Qualify for EB-2 (advanced degree or exceptional ability), then meet three NIW factors: national importance, well positioned, and waiver benefits the U.S. | Show sustained national or international acclaim: a major one-time award, or at least 3 of 10 listed criteria. No job offer required. | NIW: USCIS EB-2 page; EB-1A: USCIS EB-1 page; both viewed Nov 15, 2026; getniw.com (Nov 1, 2025; updated Mar 28, 2026) |
| Processing time (standard) | 80% of cases completed within 30 months. | 80% of cases completed within 32.5 months. | USCIS Case Processing Times, Form I-140, Service Center Operations, viewed Nov 15, 2026. |
| Approval-rate trend | Approval rate among decided cases rose from 42.5% (Q1) to 55.3% (Q3) of FY2026. Calculated from USCIS counts; not individual odds. | Approval rate among decided cases fell from 47.5% (Q1) to 32.2% (Q3) of FY2026. Calculated from USCIS counts; not individual odds. | USCIS, Form I-140 by Fiscal Year, Quarter and Case Status, FY2026 Q3 (posted Sept 8, 2026). Approvals / (approvals + denials); pending cases excluded. |
| Evidence burden | Forward-looking: describe a specific endeavor, show broader national impact beyond one employer, and show you can advance it. USCIS weighs all evidence together. | Evidence of past achievements and recognition matched to the listed criteria. No labor certification required; you may self-petition. | Same two sources as the eligibility row: USCIS EB-2 and EB-1 pages, viewed Nov 15, 2026; getniw.com (Nov 1, 2025; updated Mar 28, 2026) |
| Premium-processing window | 45 business days; fee $2,965. USCIS may approve, deny, issue a notice, or request evidence; a request for evidence restarts the clock. | 15 business days; fee $2,965. USCIS may approve, deny, issue a notice, or request evidence; a request for evidence restarts the clock. | USCIS premium-processing page, viewed Nov 15, 2026; USCIS Form G-1055 (ed. 10/26/26), p. 28. |
| Typical applicant profile | Advanced-degree professionals whose work has national reach: researchers, engineers, physicians, entrepreneurs. Some are still building toward the top of their field. Examples only. | Heavily cited researchers, professors with major grants, business leaders with national-scale achievements, award-winning artists or performers. Examples only. | getniw.com (Nov 1, 2025; updated Mar 28, 2026). Profiles are illustrative, not eligibility tests. |
| Visa queue after approval | EB-2 (all types) had about 391,000 approved petitions awaiting a visa number in June 2026, most for India-born applicants. Check the Visa Bulletin. | EB-1 (all types) had about 32,000 approved petitions awaiting a visa number in June 2026. Check the Visa Bulletin for current dates. | USCIS, Approved EB Petitions Awaiting Visa Final Priority Dates, FY2026 Q3 (as of June 2026; posted Sept 8, 2026). |

General information only, not legal advice. An approved petition is not a green card, and no category or filing strategy guarantees an outcome. The premium-processing fee is separate from, and in addition to, USCIS filing fees. Processing times exclude any visa-queue wait. FY2026 to date approval rates among decided cases: NIW 49.1%, EB-1A 40.7%.
